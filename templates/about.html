About AeroShield – PM10 Air Analysis

College-Level Machine Learning + Web Development Academic Research Project

PM10 ONLY FOCUSED

Problem Statement

"Real-world PM10 monitoring datasets for Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh contain missing observations and significant temporal variation, making PM10 analysis and forecasting challenging. A focused machine-learning framework is required to analyze historical PM10 behavior, forecast future PM10 concentration, classify pollution severity, and identify unusual PM10 observations. Therefore, this project develops a PM10-focused web-based analytical system that combines historical PM10 analysis, live PM10 monitoring, machine-learning prediction, pollution classification, and anomaly detection specifically trained on Tirupati monitoring data."

Project Aim

"To develop a web-based PM10 air-analysis system that integrates live PM10 monitoring with machine-learning-based forecasting, pollution classification, and anomaly detection using historical PM10 time-series data for Tirupati."

Academic Research Objectives

1 Analyze historical PM10 concentration patterns.
2 Study PM10 distribution and temporal behavior.
3 Identify and handle missing PM10 observations properly.
4 Develop regression models to predict future PM10 concentration.
5 Classify PM10 into pollution categories based on official standards.
6 Detect unusual PM10 observations using Isolation Forest anomaly detection.
7 Compare candidate machine-learning algorithms.
8 Evaluate model performance rigorously using chronological validation.

Official CPCB PM10 AQI Category Standards

Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB India Standard)
Category PM10 Concentration Range (µg/m³) Color Code Health Advisory Impact
Good 0 – 50 Good Minimal health impact.
Satisfactory 51 – 100 Satisfactory Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
Moderate 101 – 250 Moderate Breathing discomfort to people with lungs, asthma, and heart disease.
Poor 251 – 350 Poor Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
Very Poor 351 – 430 Very Poor Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Severe effect on sensitive groups.
Severe > 430 Severe Affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing diseases.
