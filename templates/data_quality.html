Why Missing PM10 ≠ Zero PM10

A common flaw in environmental data processing is replacing missing concentration values with zero (0 µg/m³). Zero PM10 implies perfectly pristine air without particulate matter, which is physically impossible in urban atmosphere.

Primary Causes of Missing PM10 Readings:

  • Station Maintenance & Sensor Recalibration: Scheduled zero-point checks and filter replacements.
  • Telemetry & Communication Failure: Network packet loss between station AP001 logger (Tirupati) and central server.
  • Power Outages: Regional grid interruptions at the monitoring site.
  • Data Quality Validation Rejection: Noise or voltage spikes flagged by automated QC.

Machine Learning Preprocessing Protocol

To guarantee zero target leakage and preserve data integrity across time-series modeling, AeroShield follows a strict dual-pass preprocessing workflow:

1
Target Handling: Rows with missing target values (PM10_next) are completely dropped from regression/classification training sets. They are never filled with dummy values or zeros.
2
Lag & Rolling Feature Construction: Interpolated time-series data is used strictly to compute temporal lag structures (lag_1h, rolling_mean_24h) without corrupting feature matrices.
